## 2. Information We Collect

### 2.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account or use our services, you may provide:

| Data Type | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Full Name** | To personalise your account and bookings |
| **Email Address** | Account creation via Google Sign-In; service communications |
| **Mobile Phone Number** | Booking confirmation, OTP verification, appointment reminders via WhatsApp |
| **City / Location (text)** | To display nearby barber shops in your area |
| **GPS Coordinates (latitude & longitude)** | To detect your location and show nearby shops |

### 2.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we automatically collect:

- **Firebase User ID (UID):** A unique identifier generated by Firebase Authentication when you sign in with Google
- **FCM Push Token:** A device-level token to deliver push notifications (booking confirmations, reminders, delay alerts)
- **Booking History:** Shop visited, services selected, date, time, slot status, total amount, OTP verification status
- **Payment Data:** Razorpay Order ID and Razorpay Payment ID (transaction references only — no card, bank, or UPI PIN details are stored by Cutzo)
- **Review & Rating Data:** Ratings and review text you submit for shops
- **No-Show Strike Count:** Number of missed or cancelled bookings used to enforce platform fairness policies
- **Location Request Logs:** Timestamp and GPS coordinates logged when you permit location access

### 2.3 Information from Third-Party Services

We receive certain data from:

- **Google / Firebase Authentication:** Your name, email address, and Google Account UID upon Google Sign-In
- **Razorpay Payment Gateway:** Transaction reference identifiers and payment status confirmations. We do **not** receive or store your credit/debit card numbers, bank account details, CVV, or UPI PINs
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM):** Device push token for notification delivery
- **Meta WhatsApp Business API / Twilio:** Phone number used to deliver appointment reminder messages

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use collected information to:

1. **Create and manage your account** — store your profile (name, phone, email, city) in our secure Convex database
2. **Process bookings** — record your service selection, date, time, shop, and generate a booking confirmation
3. **Enable real-time slot management** — prevent double-booking by tracking slot availability
4. **Process payments** — create and verify Razorpay payment orders for the ₹3 platform booking fee
5. **Send push notifications** — booking confirmations, delays, reminders, and updates via FCM
6. **Send appointment reminders** — via WhatsApp (Meta) or SMS (Twilio) 30 minutes before your appointment
7. **Show nearby shops** — use your GPS or city input to filter shops in your area
8. **Enable saved shops** — remember the barber shops you bookmark
9. **Display offers and coupons** — show city-specific deals relevant to you
10. **Enforce fairness policies** — track no-show strikes and impose temporary booking restrictions where necessary
11. **Send in-app notifications** — alerts about your bookings and platform updates
12. **Improve the platform** — aggregate usage and global statistics to monitor platform health
13. **Comply with legal obligations** — respond to lawful government requests, disputes, or fraud investigations

## 7. Data Security

We implement the following security measures:

- **Firebase Authentication** for secure, token-based login
- **HTTPS/TLS encryption** for all data transmitted between the App and our servers
- **HMAC-SHA256 signature verification** for all Razorpay payment events
- **Constant-time comparison** to prevent timing attacks during payment verification
- **Rate limiting** — a maximum of 5 payment order creations and 10 payment verifications per user per hour
- **Hashed passwords** — shop owner passwords are stored as bcrypt hashes (never plain text)
- **Role-based access control** — customers can only access their own data; shop owners can only manage their own shops

Despite these measures, no system is 100% secure. Please contact us immediately if you notice suspicious activity.
