5 Booking Process

5.1 How Bookings Work

  1. You browse available barber shops filtered by your location or city
  2. You select a shop, choose one or more services, pick a date and available time slot
  3. You review the booking summary and pay the ₹3 non-refundable platform booking fee via Razorpay
  4. Your booking request is sent to the shop owner for confirmation
  5. You will receive a booking confirmation notification once the shop accepts

5.2 Booking Confirmation

A booking is only confirmed when:

  • Payment of the ₹3 platform fee is successfully completed, AND
  • The shop owner accepts the booking request

A booking request that has not been accepted is not a confirmed appointment.

5.3 Slot Availability

Slot availability is checked in real time. Cutzo cannot guarantee that a slot will remain available between selection and payment. If a slot becomes unavailable before payment is completed, you will be notified and the payment will not proceed.

5.4 Service Cost

The ₹3 booking fee is a platform fee charged by Cutzo to secure your slot.

The actual cost of grooming services is displayed in the App and must be paid directly to the shop at the time of service. Cutzo does not collect the service fee.

  • The amount is hardcoded on our server — it cannot be tampered with or overridden

  • Cutzo does not store your card number, CVV, UPI PIN, or bank account details

  • A maximum of 5 payment order creations per hour per account is enforced to prevent abuse

6.3 Failed Payments

If a payment is deducted but the booking is not successfully created, the amount will be automatically refunded by Razorpay to your original payment method within 5–7 business days.
