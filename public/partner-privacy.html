1 Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Cutzo ("Cutzo", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects information in connection with the Cutzo Partner App (the "Partner App"), which is used by salon and barber-shop owners and their authorised representatives ("Partners", "you") to apply to become, and subsequently operate as, a Cutzo Partner.

Cutzo is committed to being transparent about what information it actually collects through the Partner App and why. This Policy is written specifically for the Cutzo Partner App and its actual registration, verification, and Partner-operation workflows. Wherever a specific detail could not be confirmed against the actual, current behaviour of the Partner App, this Policy marks it clearly as TO BE CONFIRMED BY CUTZO rather than assuming what a typical salon-booking app might do.

This Privacy Policy should be read together with the Cutzo Partner Terms & Conditions, which govern your use of the Partner App and the Cutzo Partner application/approval process.

2 Scope

This Policy applies to personal information processed through the Cutzo Partner App, including information collected from:

  • Individuals applying to become a Cutzo Partner ("Applicants");
  • Approved Cutzo Partners and their authorised staff/Administrators who use the Partner App; and
  • Limited Customer information that a Partner may receive through the Partner App in order to fulfil a booking.

This Policy does not separately describe the Cutzo Customer App's privacy practices, which are addressed in Cutzo's Customer Privacy Policy. Where this Policy refers to a Cutzo website (as distinct from the Partner App), the applicable practices for that website may be described separately.

The Cutzo Partner App is operated by LEGAL ENTITY NAME, having its registered office at REGISTERED OFFICE ADDRESS ("Cutzo"). For queries about this Policy or Cutzo's data practices, see Section 28 (Contact Us).

4 Information We Collect

Cutzo collects information about Applicants and Partners at different stages of the Partner relationship:

  • At initial registration/application
  • During any subsequent verification
  • After approval, in the ordinary course of operating as a Partner
  • Automatically, through use of the Partner App

Sections 5 through 9 describe each of these categories separately. This Policy does not assume that all categories described below are collected from every Applicant or at every stage.

5 Information Collected During Partner Registration

When you apply to become a Cutzo Partner, we collect the information you provide through the Partner App to create and process your application. This may include:

  • Owner/applicant name
  • Shop/salon name
  • Contact details, such as phone number and/or email address
  • Shop address and/or shop location, if requested at this stage
  • Basic account/authentication information, such as information received from a sign-in method you choose to use (see Section 14)

Registration is not the same as full business verification. At the point of initial registration, Cutzo does not necessarily collect documents such as government-issued identity documents, GST certificates, bank account details, or shop photographs unless the Partner App's current registration flow actually requests these.

6 Partner Application and Verification

Submitting a registration/application through the Partner App does not automatically make the Applicant a Cutzo Partner. After an Application is submitted, the following may occur:

  • Cutzo reviews the information submitted in the Application
  • Cutzo may contact the Applicant to ask clarifying questions or request additional information
  • Cutzo may conduct further verification, which may include contacting the Applicant and/or visiting the physical shop premises
  • Based on this review, Cutzo approves or rejects the Application

7 Information Collected After Approval

Once an Application is approved and the Applicant becomes an authorised Cutzo Partner, Cutzo may process additional information, which may include:

  • Shop information the Partner chooses to add or update, such as services offered, prices, opening hours/availability status, and shop images
  • Staff/Administrator information, where the Partner App allows the Owner to add authorised staff members to the account
  • Booking-related information necessary to manage appointments (see Section 8)
  • Account activity and usage information generated through the Partner's ordinary use of the app

8 Booking and OTP Information

Once approved, a Partner may receive information through the Partner App to manage bookings made by Customers. Depending on the Partner App's actual functionality, this may include:

  • A booking identifier
  • The Customer's name, to the extent displayed to the Partner
  • The service(s) selected by the Customer
  • The appointment date and time
  • Booking status (requested, accepted, started, completed, cancelled, or no-show)
  • OTP/check-in information used to verify the Customer's arrival for the booking

10 How We Use Information

Cutzo uses the information described in Sections 5 through 9 for the following purposes:

  • Creating and managing Partner accounts
  • Processing and reviewing Partner Applications
  • Communicating with Applicants and Partners about their Application or account status
  • Conducting verification of Applicants and Shops, including physical verification where applicable
  • Approving or rejecting Applications
  • Operating and managing approved Partners' Shop profiles and listings
  • Managing and facilitating bookings between Partners and Customers
  • Facilitating Customer check-in and OTP verification for bookings
  • Providing Customer and Partner support
  • Preventing and detecting fraud, abuse, and misuse of the Platform
  • Maintaining the security and integrity of the Partner App and the Platform
  • Troubleshooting technical issues and maintaining/improving the Partner App
  • Complying with applicable legal obligations
  • Enforcing the Cutzo Partner Terms & Conditions
  • Assisting with the resolution of disputes between Partners and Customers, or between Partners and Cutzo

  • The right to obtain a summary of the personal data being processed by Cutzo and the processing activities undertaken

  • The right to erasure of personal data that is no longer necessary for the purpose for which it was processed
  • The right to grievance redressal, as described in Section 20

These rights may be subject to reasonable exceptions and limitations under Applicable Law, for example where information must be retained to comply with a legal obligation or to establish or defend a legal claim.

24 Partner Responsibilities Regarding Customer Data

When a Partner receives Customer information through the Partner App in order to fulfil a booking (see Section 8), the Partner must:

  • Use that information only for the legitimate purpose of fulfilling the specific booking or Service to which it relates
  • Take reasonable steps to protect that information from unauthorised access, disclosure, or loss
  • Not sell that information to any third party
  • Not otherwise misuse that information
  • Not use a Customer's contact information obtained through the Platform for unauthorised marketing
  • Not share that information with any third party except as necessary to fulfil the booking, or as required by Applicable Law
  • Follow Cutzo's policies and Applicable Law, including the DPDP Act, in respect of any Customer information the Partner receives
