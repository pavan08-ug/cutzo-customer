Account & Data Deletion Request

Application: Cutzo Partner Developer: Cutzo (Cutzo Life) Last Updated: August 2026
Notice for Cutzo Partner Users: You have the right to request the complete deletion of your Cutzo Partner account and associated business data at any time. This page provides full instructions and an online request mechanism compliant with Google Play Store User Data Policies.

1 How to Request Account Deletion

Partners can submit an account deletion request through any of the following methods:

Method 1: In-App

Within the Partner App

1. Open the Cutzo Partner app.
2. Go to Profile / Settings.
3. Tap Account Security → Delete Account.
4. Confirm with OTP verification.

Method 2: Online Request

Direct Web Submission

Fill out the official request form below on this page with your registered mobile number and business name. Our team will verify and process your request within 48 hours.

Method 3: Email / Helpdesk

Email Our Support

Send an email to support@cutzolife.in with your registered mobile number and salon name.

3 Data Deletion vs. Retention Details

In accordance with Google Play developer policies and applicable privacy regulations, here is what happens to your data upon account deletion:

Data Category Action Details & Retention Policy
Shop Profile & Catalogue
Salon name, photos, address, services, prices		 Deleted Permanently removed from active servers and public customer search immediately. Scrubbed from backups within 30 days.
Partner Identity & Auth
Name, phone number, OAuth tokens, staff logins		 Deleted Account credentials and staff association links are permanently purged within 30 days.
Device & Notification Data
FCM tokens, notification history, session logs		 Deleted Purged immediately upon account deactivation.
Financial & Invoicing Records
Platform payout history, commission invoices, GST receipts		 Retained Retained strictly for legal, tax, and regulatory compliance under Indian financial laws (up to statutory limitation periods), isolated in encrypted audit storage. Not used for marketing or profiling.
Dispute & Grievance Logs
Unresolved customer refunds or support tickets		 Retained Retained temporarily until existing claims or chargebacks are legally concluded.

4 Processing Timeline

  • Immediate (0 - 24 hours): Your salon listing is deactivated and removed from the customer marketplace. You will no longer receive bookings or customer inquiries.
  • Verification (24 - 48 hours): Our support team verifies the account to ensure no pending payout or active dispute is unresolved.
  • Permanent Purge (Within 30 days): All personal and salon profile data is irreversibly wiped from active databases and database backups.
